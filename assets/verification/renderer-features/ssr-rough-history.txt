SSR rough-history filtering — 2026-09-09

Scope and decision
The user identified the reflective Bistro bar as the primary flicker repro and
approved temporal-clamp and roughness-filter tuning within the existing ray,
texture-read and image budgets, accepting softness and short trails.

Selected implementation
History clamping varies continuously with roughness, from full current bounds at
the mirror threshold to unclamped accumulation at roughness 0.25. This removes the
former two-to-three-hit discontinuity: identical current/history colors 10/2 and
coverage 1 changed output 6 to 10 solely because the supported-neighbor count changed.
The new rough branch produces 3.2 in both cases at configured weight 0.85.

Selected roughness and existing unjittered motion determine history weight.
Stationary rough receivers use 0.95 at defaults; one full-resolution source pixel
of motion per frame returns to 0.85. Mirrors retain their configured weight and
one source tap; their sparse temporal clamp is stricter than the former allowance.
Empty neighborhoods fade valid covered radiance and coverage together. Invalid
receiver depth/identity still rejects history immediately. At 0.95 the history
half-life is 13.5 rendered frames; moving reflected objects on a static receiver
can trail because receiver motion does not identify reflected-object motion.

SSR spatial/composite depth tolerance is max(0.02 m, center_depth*0.05), independent
of ray thickness. The previous 0.1 m minimum admitted chrome/trim separated by 8 cm.
The scalar helper parameter preserves SSGI's previous minimum without changing its
filtering policy. No new images, rays, root fields or texture reads were added.

An optional skip of nine unused clamp reads was not selected. Its raw captures
were identical, but a few history values elsewhere differed and equivalence was
not established. The selected code retains the original loops. Timing from that
reverted version is excluded; its profile overlapped CPU artifact verification.
