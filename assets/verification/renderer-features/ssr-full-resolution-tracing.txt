Retained implementation
Each source pixel owns its ray, raw incoming radiance and integer hit record.
The independent depth pyramid remains floor-half, with full-resolution leaves,
odd trailing rows/columns and the 48-decision limit. Remove the redundant receiver
coordinate image and its depth-base/trace/temporal bindings on both backends.

Rough temporal gathers use nine source pixels at offsets {-2,0,2} on each axis,
weighted at half those offsets to retain approximately the previous filter width.
Mirrors use their exact center ray. Four independently validated history taps,
receiver/hit identities, both depths, selected normals, incoming-radiance shading
and unsupported-hit fallback remain. No old reflection survives without support.

The immediate-neighbor full-resolution trial narrowed the filter and improved
bar-lip display variation only 24.6%. The retained wider footprint improves it
34.5%. Both trials are recorded separately in ssr-full-resolution-tracing.json.
