Rejected MetalFX scale-dependent jitter trial — 2026-09-09

Decision
The trial increases static display variation in the Bistro reflective-bar ROI.
It is retained as diagnostic evidence only. It does not establish a rendering
fix, performance result, cross-backend result, or baseline candidate.

Static result
Both static snapshots pass. They are 1280x720 Metal/MetalFX captures at 80%
render scale, manual exposure 8, SSR and GTAO enabled, SSGI/bloom disabled.
Their historical top-level reports state the rounded configured fallback
1024x576, but the captured raw source channels are 1025x577. The trapezoid
[(0,253),(1107,249),(1080,283),(0,378)] contains 86,265 output pixels. Each
value is the maximum per-pixel RGB range across the 24 captured checkpoints 3..26.

  final_color                         before       after       delta
  mean maximum RGB range              0.746641     0.804208    +0.057567
  p99 maximum RGB range               3 codes      4 codes     +1 code
  maximum RGB range                   11 codes     13 codes    +2 codes
  pixels with range over 3 codes      859          1475        +616 (+71.7%)

The intermediate channels do not reverse that result: hdr_pre_bloom pixels over
0.001 HDR increase 355 to 539; hdr_post_transmission increases 9,679 to 9,951;
ssr_reflection remains 56. The output regression is sufficient to reject the
trial for this static ROI.

Limits
The reports are local, dirty, non-authoritative snapshots with no baseline.
The two binaries differ, and camera-script version changes from 5 to 6, so this
is evidence against the complete dirty experiment rather than an isolated A/B
attribution. GPU timing was disabled in every snapshot; no timing claim follows.
The 24 static samples are not a full 64-phase noise cycle. Native Vulkan,
cross-backend parity, Metal API/shader validation, and dynamic-resolution
transition evidence were not run for this trial.

The four completed snapshot directories 20260909T084000.931Z-00b34b,
20260909T084804.625Z-00dc3c, 20260909T084510.233Z-00d046 and
20260909T085402.363Z-00f93a were retired after their report digests were checked
against this record. This text and PNG preserve the decisive result. Earlier
