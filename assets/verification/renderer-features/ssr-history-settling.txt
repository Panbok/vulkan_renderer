SSR history fading and static accumulation — 2026-09-09

Result and limits
-----------------
The approved 128-frame SSR settling window precedes the existing 128-sample
static TAA/FSR integral. The tested Bistro bar becomes identical in final color
and reconstructed HDR after that sequence. Early camera-stop differences fall,
but intermittent reflections are weaker and some early pixel outliers increase.
MetalFX flicker remains unresolved. This is local Metal evidence, not native
Vulkan parity, an all-scene quality result, or a performance claim.

The separate no-TAA pass-capacity correction and its diagnostics are recorded in
ssr-no-taa-capacity.txt (commit 1bc546e5). Stale CPU capture/display fixture
contracts were corrected separately in commit 25b742bb.

Implemented policy
------------------
- Empty SSR neighborhoods retain at most half their validated coverage each
  frame; conditional RGB stays unchanged. Rejected depth/identity clears.
- Supported updates preserve in-bound temporal averaging, while at most half
  the normalized RGB residual outside current bounds survives, including sparse
  current coverage. Mirrors retain their hard clamp. Broad bounds can still
  preserve stale reflected detail; receiver identity cannot prove hit identity.
- With SSR enabled, the first 128 matching submitted scene frames remain on the
  ordinary temporal path. Match 129 starts the existing 128-sample static mean.
  At 60 fps, settling plus accumulation takes about 4.3 seconds.
- One uint32 counter belongs to each native CPU scene-history record; padding
  grows that record from 48 to 56 bytes. The selected producer owns the count.
  Invalid history, changed scene/resource/graph state, or an SSR toggle restarts
  the window. Only successful submission publishes prepared metadata.
- Vulkan TAA and FSR preparation/stabilization use the same readiness rule.
  During settling, FSR keeps normal composition masking and current SDK output.
  SSR-off scenes retain immediate static accumulation. MetalFX is unchanged.
- No new images, rays, roots, bindings, history instances or shader read ceilings.
